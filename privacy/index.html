Privacy

LAST REVIEWED 2026-11-11 · HONG KONG

THE SHORT VERSION

This site collects nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no tracking cookies, no session storage of your inputs, and no backend. GitHub Pages serves the files; it keeps its own standard server access logs, which I do not control or inspect.

THE CONTACT FORM

The form does not transmit anything. When you press OPEN EMAIL DRAFT ▸, it builds a mailto: link containing the name, email, service type and message you typed, and opens it in your own mail application. You choose whether to send it. Nothing is stored on this site, and no copy exists until your mail client sends the message to me.

If you send it, I use that information only to reply about your store. I do not add you to a mailing list, sell it, or share it with third parties. There is no mailing list.

THE 50 TOOLS

Every tool in the armory runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Inputs you type into a tool never leave your machine — there is no upload, no account, and no network request carrying your data. Some tools store your own preferences locally in your browser so settings survive a reload; that data stays on your device.

LANGUAGE PREFERENCE

The site remembers your chosen language in your browser's localStorage. That value is read only by your browser and never sent to a server.

THIRD PARTIES

All fonts, stylesheets, scripts and images are hosted on this domain. There are no third-party font, analytics, CDN or advertising requests. External links (to client stores, the source repository, or GitHub) take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete a message you previously sent: use the form on the contact page. Replies are usually within one day.
